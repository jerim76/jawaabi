Jawaab Empowerment Initiative (JEI) is a community-based organization working to advance mental wellness, climate action, gender equity and access to clean water and sanitation for all.

WHO WE ARE

Creating opportunities for stronger communities

Jawaab Empowerment Initiative works with communities to address social, environmental and economic challenges while creating opportunities for people to live healthier and more dignified lives.

We believe that sustainable change happens when communities are empowered to identify their own challenges and participate in creating solutions.
